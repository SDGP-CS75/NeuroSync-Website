Privacy notice.

This notice explains how the NeuroSync website and Android beta download flow handle information.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

The Android beta is an early build. Do not enter sensitive health information until a complete app-specific privacy notice is published.

This website

This website does not include advertising scripts, third-party analytics, or its own account or email signup form. It does not set its own cookies.

The site is delivered through Vercel. Vercel may process standard technical request data such as an IP address, browser details, and request time to deliver and secure the site.

Downloads

The current APK is hosted by Expo EAS. When you download it, Expo may process standard technical request data under its own policies. The website does not receive your name, email address, or health data as part of the download.

Feedback links

Support and beta feedback links open GitHub. Anything you post in a GitHub issue is public and is handled under GitHub's privacy terms. Never post passwords, health records, or other sensitive information.

Android app data

The beta app may store information locally on your device while you test it. Use Android settings to clear the app's data or uninstall it when you want to remove that local information. A full app privacy notice will be required before public release.

Questions

Start with the support page. For a privacy question, open a GitHub issue without including personal or health information so the team can arrange a safer way to respond.
